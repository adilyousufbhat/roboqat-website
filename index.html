    For parents

    Questions parents ask

    Can’t find your answer? Email us at info@roboqat.com and we will help.

    What ages do you teach?

    Children aged 6 to 12. Level 1 is for ages 6–8, Level 2 for ages 8–10 and Level 3 for ages 10–12.

    Does my child need any experience?

    No. Level 1 starts from the very basics. Children with some coding or robotics experience can start at a higher level.

    What language are classes taught in?

    All classes are taught in English.

    How long is each level?

    Level 1 runs for 8 weeks, Level 2 for 10 weeks and Level 3 for 12 weeks. Each level ends with a showcase, a challenge or a championship.

    What is the Robot Battle Arena?

    A supervised after-class activity for enrolled students, in 30-minute sessions. Children test the robots they build in friendly, controlled matches.

    How will I know how my child is doing?

    You receive progress updates and photos of your child’s projects on WhatsApp, and you are invited to showcases and a parent open day every term.

    How much are the fees?

    Fees depend on the level and the package you choose. Email us at info@roboqat.com and we will send you the current options.

    Is there an offer for new students?

    Yes. Enrol now and get 10% off your first month.

    Where are you and when are you open?

    We are at Unit 105, The Pearl-Qatar, Doha, Qatar. We are open Sunday to Thursday from 2:00 to 6:00 PM and on Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Friday.

    Do you work with schools?

    Yes. We offer STEM demo days, after-school clubs, school taster weeks and holiday camps. See the schools section above, or contact us.
