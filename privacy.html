Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

ROBOQAT COMPUTER TRAINING CENTER W.L.L. (C.R. No. 189854) (“RoboQAT”, “we”) runs this website, roboqat.com. This page explains, in plain words, what information we receive and how we use it.

What this website collects

This website does not collect or store personal information. It has no user accounts, no payment forms and no database. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

The contact form does not send anything to us by itself. When you press “Send by email”, it opens your own email app with a message to info@roboqat.com, and you decide whether to send it.

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the “Get directions” link opens Google Maps. When you use them, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, under Google’s own privacy policy.

What you send us

If you contact us by email or by message, we receive what you choose to send, for example your name, your phone number, your child’s age and your question.

  • We use it only to reply to you and to arrange classes for your child.
  • We do not sell it or share it with anyone else for marketing.
  • We keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us. Just email us and we will help.

Changes to this page

If the way we handle information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
